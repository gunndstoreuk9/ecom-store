## Logo Direction

Header logo must be RTL-first:

- On the far right: a circular monogram with the letter **N** inside.
- Circle color: primary brand blue.
- Next to it: Arabic wordmark **تَوازُن للصحة** in a premium Arabic font.
- Below Arabic wordmark: small English line **Nama Beauty**.
- Keep the logo compact on mobile.

Recommended typography:

- Arabic display: `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`, `Noto Kufi Arabic`, or `Cairo`.
- Arabic body: `Tajawal` or `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`.
- Latin: `Inter`.

## Strategic Position

Tawazon is not a generic supplement seller. Tawazon is a specialist Moroccan DTC health brand focused on sugar-balance nutrition.

Internal positioning statement:

> For Moroccan adults who are worried about rising sugar, cravings, fatigue, and losing control of their health, Tawazon Health is the specialist brand that helps support healthy blood-sugar balance already within the normal range, steady energy, and calmer cravings through an American formula with 20 plant and mineral actives, strong proof, COD safety, and human follow-up.

- GMP
- Non-GMO
- Halal, only if certificate is real and available
- Lab-tested / COA, only if certificate is real and available
- 60 كبسولة
- 20 active ingredients
- 30-day support course
- الدفع عند الاستلام
- ضمان 30 يوم
- دعم واتساب مغربي

## Emotional Promise

The emotional purchase is not just "lower sugar." It is:

- feeling in control again
- peace of mind after scary lab results
- fewer afternoon crashes
- less fear of family diabetes history
- doing something responsible for the family
- avoiding shame by choosing a natural, halal, imported support product

Core line:

**توازن السكر، توازن الحياة**

Support lines:

- راحة البال تبدأ من خطوة صغيرة يومياً.
- ماشي ضعف إرادة. الجسم يحتاج دعماً ذكياً لتوازن السكر والطاقة.
- ادفع فقط عند الاستلام، وجرب كور التوازن بثقة.

## Proof Architecture

Use proof in this order:

1. Ingredient proof: 20 actives and hero ingredients.
2. Manufacturing proof: USA, GMP, Non-GMO, COA, Halal.
3. Mechanism proof: Tawazon Balance Matrix.
4. Human proof: pharmacist/doctor review.
5. Customer proof: Moroccan photo reviews, WhatsApp screenshots, city names.
6. Operational proof: COD, WhatsApp, guarantee, clear phone support.
